## 1. What Catch is

A training-log and analysis tool for rowers and other endurance athletes. It imports data from services you connect, computes recovery, strain and
training insights, offers an optional AI coach that uses your own AI provider key, and (public deployment) optional public leaderboards and clubs.

pay for AI and is not responsible for the provider's charges, availability, outputs or handling of data. See the AI data-sharing explanation.

Concept2 — not that Catch vouches for the person who rowed it.
