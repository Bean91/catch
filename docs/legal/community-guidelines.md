# Community guidelines — DRAFT

> **DRAFT — NOT LEGALLY REVIEWED.** Applies to the public features: leaderboards and clubs.

1. **Be honest.** Publish only results that are yours. A result's "verified" label means its source was authenticated, not that anyone vouches for you.
2. **Be kind.** No harassment, hate, threats, sexual content, or shaming of anyone — especially young athletes.
3. **Protect people.** Don't share anyone's private information, real name (without their say), contact details, location, or training data. Don't try
   to identify someone behind a handle or "Anonymous rower".
4. **Keep it about rowing.** No spam, adverts, scams or links used to pull people elsewhere. Junior clubs have no links in descriptions or rules.
5. **Protect young people.** Junior clubs are private, approved by a coach, and have no direct messages. Adults must not use Catch to contact young
   athletes outside a junior club's coach-approved channels. If you are worried about someone's safety, report it.
6. **Don't game it.** No fake accounts, no mass reporting, no evading a decision by re-posting.

## What happens

Anyone can report a result, post, member, club or challenge. Three reports from accounts at least a week old hide a result or post for review; a
person (a platform moderator, who is not a member of the club in question) then decides. Club staff can act inside their own club with a written
reason; every action is logged. If your content is hidden or your account restricted, you are told what and why, and you can appeal once;
a different moderator reviews it. A restriction never stops you downloading or deleting your own data.
