# Not medical advice — DRAFT

> **DRAFT — NOT LEGALLY REVIEWED.**

Catch is a training tool, not a medical device or a health-care provider. Its recovery, strain, sleep, illness early-warning, body-composition,
heart-rate-zone, fuelling and injury-related outputs are **estimates from consumer-device data and simple models**. They can be wrong, and they are
not a diagnosis, treatment, or a substitute for a doctor, physiotherapist, coach or dietitian.

* If you feel unwell, have chest pain, dizziness, an injury, or symptoms that worry you, stop training and seek medical advice — whatever the app says.
* The illness early-warning is a prompt to look after yourself, not a test for illness. It can miss things and flag things that are nothing.
* Calorie and macro figures are estimates, including anything read from a photo. Under-fuelling flags are informational. If you have or suspect an
  eating disorder, please talk to a professional.
* The AI coach can make mistakes and is told not to diagnose; do not rely on it for health decisions. Check anything that matters.
* Young athletes: training load and nutrition decisions should involve a parent/guardian and qualified coach.

You use the information at your own risk. **[counsel: jurisdiction-specific wording]**
