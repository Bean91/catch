# Deleting your Catch account

> Draft text for the product team. It has not had legal review and is not a compliance statement.

You can delete your Catch account and the data in it at any time, from the app or from this page.

## In the app (iPhone, Android or web)

1. Open **Settings** and scroll to **Account** (or open **Settings → Privacy**).
2. Choose **Delete account…**.
3. Type `DELETE` to confirm. If you signed in more than ten minutes ago, Catch asks you to sign in again first, so nobody else can delete your account from an unlocked phone.

## If you can't sign in

Use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in screen to regain access, then follow the steps above. If you no longer have access to the email address on the account, write to the contact address in the privacy policy from an address you can prove is yours and we will help.

## What is deleted

Your sign-in, workouts, wellness and body data, nutrition and gym logs, notes, photos you attached, coach conversations and memory, connected-source credentials, push-notification registrations and everything you imported from Apple Health or Health Connect. Garmin, Withings, Concept2 and Whoop connections are removed from Catch; where the provider documents a way to revoke Catch's access, Catch uses it, and you can also revoke access in that provider's own settings.

Public results you published to a leaderboard are removed. Clubs you own are handed to another adult member, or deleted if there is none. Moderation records are kept without your account identifier, because a moderation decision has to stay explainable.

## What happens next

Deletion starts immediately and is not reversible. Backups of the database are overwritten on the normal backup cycle (see the data-deletion policy for the period).

You can also delete one kind of data without closing the account (Settings → Privacy), and stop sharing and delete what was imported from Apple Health or Health Connect (Settings → Connections → Health).
