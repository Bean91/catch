# Health and fitness policy review (what the rules require and how Catch meets them) — read from public policy text; not legal advice

**Apple (App Review Guidelines 1.4.1, 5.1.1, 5.1.2, 5.1.3, 2.5.1):** health data from HealthKit may not be used for advertising or sold; it must not be stored in iCloud; apps that use HealthKit must be about health/fitness and say so in their description; usage strings must be specific; data collected must be disclosed and consent obtained; account deletion must be possible in-app (5.1.1(v)); no medical-accuracy claims without evidence (the app states it isn't medical advice).
→ read-only (`NSHealthShareUsageDescription` only), consent recorded before the OS sheet, a preview before any upload, in-app deletion, "not medical advice" in listing and legal drafts, no ad/analytics SDKs, no iCloud. HealthKit capability only needed for the signed build.
**Google Play (Health Connect + Health apps declaration):** declare each Health Connect permission with a user-visible justification; only request what a feature uses; show a privacy policy from the permission screen; data can't be used for ads or sold; account deletion (in-app + web URL); Data safety form accurate; sensitive-permission declarations.
→ the merged manifest declares only the seven read permissions (the plugin's ~40 are removed); the plugin's rationale activity (`ACTION_SHOW_PERMISSIONS_RATIONALE` / `VIEW_PERMISSION_USAGE`) is merged in — `health_connect_privacy_policy_url` in `strings.xml` points at `/legal/privacy-policy` (public, signed-out reachable); deletion URL `/account-deletion`.
**Both:** Apple 4.2 (minimum functionality) and Google "webview-only app" expectations — see app-review-notes.md. Photo uses the system picker (limited access works). Push is opt-in. Age: Catch has junior-club features (sessions 26/27) with minors handled server-side; age rating should be set accordingly (the form questions about user-generated content and chat apply: clubs have announcements, reports and moderation).
