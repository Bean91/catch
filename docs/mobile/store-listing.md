**Description**
Catch is a training app for rowers. It brings your sessions and recovery together — Garmin, Concept2, Withings and Whoop, plus Apple Health or Health Connect if you choose — and turns them into a daily recovery score, training strain, erg test and 2k projections, a gym logger that works with no signal, a calendar with race planning, and an AI coach you power with your own API key.

• Recovery, sleep, HRV and strain on one screen, with the inputs behind each number shown.
• A strength logger built for the gym floor: one-handed, offline-first, with rest timer, plate calculator and PR detection. Sets are kept on your phone until they sync.
• Erg tests, pacing plans and PM5 photo capture — point the camera at the monitor and review the splits.
• Calendar, races and training phases; workout library; technique notes.
• Notifications you control: a morning brief, illness/overtraining warnings, sync alerts, race reminders — each switchable, with quiet hours.
• Optional Apple Health / Health Connect import: you choose the data types, preview exactly what would be sent, and can delete it at any time.
• Your data stays yours: export or delete it in the app, delete your account in two taps.

Catch is not a medical device and gives no medical advice.
